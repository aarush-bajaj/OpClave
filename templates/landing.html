Question
What's the most important thing to understand about consciousness?
Synthesis
The hard problem. All four models converge on the same central mystery: it's not whether brains produce consciousness, but why any physical process gives rise to subjective experience at all. GPT and Claude emphasize the explanatory gap: we can map neural correlates precisely without ever explaining why there's "something it is like" to be aware. Gemini argues this may require entirely new scientific frameworks beyond classical neuroscience. Grok takes the sharpest angle: the question itself might be malformed; our intuitions about the mind/body divide could be the actual source of confusion, not a clue toward an answer.

How it works

Simple by design.

01
Ask your question

Type anything: a hard question, a decision you're wrestling with, a concept you want explained from multiple angles. Attach a file if you have one.

02
Voyance responds

All four models answer independently and in parallel. You get each perspective in its own tab, unfiltered, unmerged, and fully attributable to its source.

03
One synthesis

A synthesis model reads everything and produces a unified answer, capturing consensus, surfacing conflict, and showing you what no single model would have said on its own.
