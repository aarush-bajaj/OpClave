Question
RAG or fine-tuning: which should you use for a production LLM application?
Synthesis
Start with RAG. All four models converge on the same default recommendation, but diverge sharply on when to move to fine-tuning. GPT and Claude both argue that most teams reach for fine-tuning prematurely, when what they actually need is better retrieval or prompting. Gemini flags the infrastructure cost: a fine-tuned model requires ongoing versioning, retraining, and drift monitoring. Grok takes the sharpest angle: the real question is not RAG versus fine-tuning, but whether your problem is a knowledge problem or a behavior problem. Most teams misdiagnose this. If your use case involves dynamic data or requires auditability, RAG wins by default. Fine-tuning earns its place only when you need to reshape how the model reasons or responds, not what it knows.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Four models, one query

Send your question once and get independent responses from GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok in parallel. No tab switching, no copy-pasting. See exactly where they agree and where they don't.

02
Voyance synthesis

A fifth model reads all four responses and produces one unified answer, surfacing where the models converge, flagging where they diverge, and finding what no single model said alone.

Research & Review

Generate structured research documents with citations, or audit code for bugs, security, and quality. Paste, upload, or connect a GitHub repository for a full multi-model review.

Every Studio output is saved automatically. Open any artifact, export it as Markdown or HTML, or follow up with a new prompt. Attach a saved document to your next session for full context.

How it works

Simple by design.

Voyance
01
Ask your question

Type anything: a hard question, a decision you're wrestling with, a concept you want explained from multiple angles. Attach a file if you have one.

02
Four models respond

GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok answer independently and in parallel. Each perspective lives in its own tab, unfiltered and fully attributable to its source.

03
One synthesis

A synthesis model reads all four responses and produces a unified answer, capturing consensus, surfacing conflict, and showing you what no single model would have said on its own.

Studio
01
Pick a mode

Choose Build, Research, or Review and give a prompt. Be as specific or as broad as you want. Attach a saved artifact to give the models full context from your previous work.

02
The council collaborates

The models work simultaneously, each contributing different strengths: implementations for code, independent findings for research, parallel audits for review.

03
Complete artifact

A synthesis model composes a single polished artifact, resolving conflicts and combining the best of each. The result is saved to your library, ready to follow up on.
